SPEED BOOST
=== P47 ===
Gameplay Benefits
• Prior speed cap limited range of viable player speed
• 22 – 30m/s
• Uncapping speed allows more natural motion
• 45-60m/s base cap
• 100m/s max speed
• Fall speed can translate more directly to horizontal speed
• Increases skill ceiling as variety of moves can get 
meaningful rewards
